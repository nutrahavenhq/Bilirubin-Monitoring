▤   Interpretation & Referral

Prototype only — NOT for clinical decisions. Thresholds are transcribed from supplied tables and not independently clinically validated. Whole-hour 6–96 h values are supplied for ≥35 weeks; ages above 96 hours display the 96-hour maximum row as a user-requested, clinically unvalidated rule. Fractional-hour interpolation is experimental. Preterm ages 23–34 weeks use discrete rows. This tool does not assess acute bilirubin encephalopathy or all clinical modifiers. Data are processed locally in this browser and not intentionally transmitted or saved.
